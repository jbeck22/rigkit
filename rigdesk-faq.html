Getting started

What is Rigdesk?#

Rigdesk is the director's desk for a multi-camera shoot. Link iPhones running the free Rigcam app, see every camera live, choose which one is on air, control each camera, and start and stop them all together. After each take, the full-quality clips copy to your iPad.

What do I need?#

An iPad or iPhone on iOS 26 or later running Rigdesk, plus one or more iPhones running Rigcam. Rigdesk has a compact layout for iPhone. No account is needed.

How many cameras can I use?#

Two on the free version, and up to four with RigKit Pro. If you add a third camera on the free version, Rigdesk says "The free version links 2 cameras. RigKit Pro links 4."

How do I start a session?#

The first time, Rigdesk asks for Local Network access and lets you set the name phones will see. On the home screen, type a name under Session and tap New session.

How do I add cameras?#

  1. In the session, tap + (Add cameras), or tap an empty camera tile.
  2. On each iPhone, open Rigcam, tap the connect button and pick this iPad.
  3. The phone appears under Nearby Rigcam Phones with a six-digit code. Check that it matches the phone's code, then tap Accept (or Reject).

Paired phones are listed under Known devices and reconnect on their own next time.

Directing

How do I switch which camera is on air?#

Tap a camera tile to put it on air. Its border turns red and the phone shows ON AIR. To adjust a camera without putting it on air, touch and hold its tile. The selected camera has a blue border.

How do I record?#

Tap the record button to start every camera together on a shared clock, and tap it again to stop. By default, only the iPad starts and stops recording. To let a phone start or stop the take too, open Session format (the gear) and choose A camera may also request a start or stop.

What can I control on each camera?#

With a camera selected, the bottom bar lets you:

  • Adjust Exposure, Focus and White Balance, each with Auto and Reset.
  • Zoom and switch lenses.
  • Take photo on that camera.
  • Switch between the front and back cameras.

The More controls (…) menu has Photo on every camera, Tint, Light on/off, Identify this camera and Show/Hide guides.

To pick a different camera, use the Selected menu or touch and hold its tile.

How do I set resolution, frame rate and codec?#

Tap the gear (Session format). Choose:

  • Resolution: 1080p, 4K or 4K DCI
  • Orientation: Landscape or Portrait
  • Frame rate: 24, 25, 30 or 60
  • Codec: HEVC, or ProRes LT and ProRes 422 (Pro)
  • Color: Rec. 709, or HLG (Pro)

Every camera uses this format when it can. A camera that can't joins with its closest option and shows a format badge on its tile. You can't change the format while recording.

Why does the live picture look softer than my recording?#

The grid shows a lower-resolution preview so switching stays quick. The full-quality masters copy to the iPad after you stop the take.

What do the badges on each tile mean?#

Each tile shows the camera's slot and name (for example CAM 1 · Jeramey's iPhone), link strength, battery and recording status. Hot means the phone is running hot. A format badge means that camera records a different format from the session. Reconnecting… or Not connected means the link dropped.

How do I end a session?#

Tap the back arrow (End session) and confirm. The phones are released. Clips that already copied stay on the iPad.

Takes and files

Where are my clips?#

Tap the stack icon (Takes and photos). Each take lists every camera's clip with its copy progress (waiting for the phone, copying, copied). Tap a file to preview it, or use Share or Save to Photos. You can also use Share all or Save all to Photos. The files are also in the Files app under On My iPad > Rigdesk (or On My iPhone > Rigdesk) in a folder named after the session.

What is the switched video?#

With RigKit Pro, Rigdesk builds one video for each take that follows the camera switches you made while recording. It appears at the top of the take as Switched video once every camera's clip has copied. If it fails, tap Try again. On the free version, the take shows Switched video · RigKit Pro.

How do I see an earlier session?#

On the home screen under Recent sessions, tap a session to see its clips and photos, save them to Photos or delete the session.

How do I delete a session?#

Open the session from Recent sessions and tap Delete session and its clips, then confirm. This removes the files from this device only and can't be undone.

Purchases (RigKit Pro)

What does RigKit Pro add?#

Up to four cameras in a session, ProRes recording, HDR (HLG) recording, and a switched video made from every take. The free version has two cameras, no watermark and no time limit.

How do I buy it?#

On the Rigdesk home screen, tap RigKit Pro: four cameras, ProRes, HDR, then pick a plan. Pro-only choices in Session format also open this screen.

Do I need to buy Pro on every phone?#

No. Rigcam is always free. You buy Pro once in Rigdesk, and it covers every camera linked to that Rigdesk. One purchase covers every device on your Apple Account.

Does Family Sharing work?#

Yes. RigKit Pro supports Family Sharing, so family members in your Family Sharing group can use it too.

I bought Pro but it isn't active.#

Open the RigKit Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. When Pro is active, the screen says "Pro is active on this Apple Account. Thank you." If no purchase is found, make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel?#

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel. Cancel in your iPhone or iPad Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions at least a day before the period ends. The Lifetime purchase never renews.

Privacy

Does Rigdesk collect my video?#

No. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Video goes only between your own devices, encrypted, and never to us. If you buy RigKit Pro, Apple processes the payment, and RevenueCat receives a random app user ID and your purchase status so Rigdesk knows Pro is active.

Troubleshooting

A phone doesn't show up.#

In Add cameras, tap Can't find your phone?:

  • Rigcam must be open and in front on the phone.
  • Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on both devices, and turn off Personal Hotspot.
  • Both apps need Local Network access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.
  • Stay within about 10 m (33 ft).

Then tap Restart discovery. The phones don't need to be on the same Wi-Fi network as the iPad.

"Not enough space on this device".#

The iPad didn't have room for that clip, so it stays on the phone. Free up space on the iPad. The clip is still in Rigcam on that phone.

"Stop recording before changing the format."#

The session format can't change during a take. Stop recording, then open Session format.

The prices didn't load on the RigKit Pro screen.#

Check your internet connection and tap Try again.
