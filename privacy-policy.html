Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Rigcam (for iPhone) and Rigdesk (for iPad), together "the apps", are operated by Jeramey Beck ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the apps handle and what leaves your devices.

In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your video, audio and photos stay on your own devices. The only information that reaches a service outside your devices is purchase information, if you buy RigKit Pro.

What stays on your devices

Video, audio and photos. Rigcam records with the iPhone's camera and microphone. Recordings and photos are stored on that iPhone. When the phone is linked to an iPad running Rigdesk, a live preview and, after each take, the recorded files are sent directly from the phone to that iPad over your local network. This transfer is encrypted and goes only between your devices. We never receive, see or store your recordings.

Device names and pairing. To link devices, the apps find each other on your local network and exchange a device name, a six-digit pairing code and a device identity key. These stay on the paired devices.

Settings and session lists. Session names, formats and preferences are stored on the device.

What we collect

Purchase information (Rigdesk only). If you buy RigKit Pro, the payment is processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. To know whether Pro is active, Rigdesk uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random app user identifier, your purchase and subscription status, and basic technical information such as app version, device model, operating system version and country. This is not linked to your name or email by us.

Rigcam contains no purchases and sends nothing to us or to RevenueCat.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking across apps and websites
  • No video, audio, photos, or location
  • No contacts or other device content

Permissions the apps ask for

  • Camera and Microphone (Rigcam): to record video and sound.
  • Local Network (both apps): to find and connect the phones and the iPad.
  • Photos, add only (both apps): to save a recording or photo to your photo library when you ask.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. The only third parties involved are Apple (payments and app delivery) and RevenueCat (purchase status), as described above.

Keeping and deleting data

Recordings and settings remain on your devices until you delete them or delete the apps. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to provide the purchase. To ask for deletion of purchase status data held by RevenueCat, contact us.

Children

The apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
