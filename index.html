Rigcam and Rigdesk

Rigcam turns each iPhone into a camera. Rigdesk, on an iPad, shows every camera live, switches which one is on air, sets focus, exposure and zoom, and starts and stops recording on all of them at once.

Getting started

  • Install Rigcam on each iPhone and Rigdesk on the iPad. Put them on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • In Rigdesk, start a session and choose Add cameras. In Rigcam, pick the iPad and enter the six-digit code it shows.
  • Allow the Camera, Microphone and Local Network permissions when asked. Without Local Network the devices cannot find each other.
  • Tap a camera to select it, tap again to put it on air, and use the record button to roll every camera together.
  • After a take stops, each camera's full-quality clip copies to the iPad. Keep the apps open until the copies finish.

Pricing

Both apps are free. Rigcam is fully free. Rigdesk is free with two cameras, with no watermark and no time limit.

RigKit Pro is one optional upgrade bought in Rigdesk. It adds up to four cameras, ProRes recording, HDR (HLG) recording, and a single switched video made from every take. It is offered as a monthly or yearly subscription with a free trial, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Current prices are shown in the app.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Manage or cancel in Settings, under your Apple Account, Subscriptions.

Common questions

  • A phone does not appear in Rigdesk. Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network and that Local Network is allowed for both apps in Settings.
  • I bought Pro but it is not active. Open the RigKit Pro screen in Rigdesk and choose Restore purchases.
  • Where are my recordings? On each phone in Rigcam's takes list, and on the iPad in Rigdesk's takes list once copied. From there you can save them to Photos.
